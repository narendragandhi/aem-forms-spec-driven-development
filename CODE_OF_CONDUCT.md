# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and constructive community.

Participants must not engage in harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, or deliberate disruption. Give feedback about ideas and code, assume good intent, and help keep technical discussions accessible to people with different backgrounds.

Maintainers may remove contributions or restrict participation when behavior violates this standard. Report concerns privately to the repository maintainers rather than escalating them in public threads.
